🥇 x 2 Hackathon wins in a week!

Group 2 consisting of the SMC Student Emil Zawistowski, the SSD Jinpeng Zhang and the CS-Software BSc student Alberte Lohse won the [Techathon - Sydhavnen 2025](https://twilight-anteater-fb4.notion.site/Techathon-Sydhavnen-2025-183a7b7ac4fe4dee8d98bea971f9480f) today. 

They addressed the challenge of 24/7 System for Recyclables, specfically "Design a system for around the clock drop-off and pickup that keeps items intact for appropriate waste sorting or rehoming." Emil used a YOLO-based object detection system to identify recyclable items, Alberte developed a pathfinding system with a graphical UI, while Jinpeng provided the situational awareness with a 3D model of the recycling station. CUER collective congratulates the winners! Emil thus fulfilled the mini-project requirements of the MLME course and gained exam right.

A week ago, [the cuer collective intern Martin Bivolov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martin-bivolov-10bb8b188) & team won the [mini hackathon organized by the BEC Financial Technologies](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7395136949122043904). Congratulations to them as well!
