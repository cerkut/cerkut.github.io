I have earned a digital credential for working knowledge of AIStor's native OpenSharing implementation, the feature that lets table data be shared securely with external consumers such as Databricks.

The credential covers:

- end-to-end table sharing with external consumers
- presigned URLs
- IAM policies and token permissions
- lifecycle management
- protection of Delta and Iceberg tables
- monitoring shared table activity with audit logs and Prometheus metrics
